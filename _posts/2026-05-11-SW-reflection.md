## My Reflection Paragraph

Over the course of the month of April and into May, I collaborated with three team members to make a website for Ms. Nora Simmons to manage the collection and distribution of med school application information. Ms. Simmons would create Microsoft Notebooks for each eligible faculty member and pre-med student. This manual notebook creation would take hours to complete, and adding information to each notebook was even more hassle. Thus, we sought to automate this process with Razor Pages, provided through the ASP.NET Entity Framework. Together, as a team, we created a website that allows Ms. Simmons to make faculty and student profiles, and easily manage the interaction between the two profiles. Then, she and the recommendation letter team can use the evaluations from faculty to write letters on the behalf of students. These letters can be stored on the website and sent to any school that the student selects. While there is still work to be done before the site is fully functional, by applying the tools we acquired in Databases and Web Systems, we developed a strong prototype that significantly reduces the hours Ms. Simmons spent on manual notebook creation, allowing her to prioritize more impactful advocacy for her students.
